MARADMIN Research Desk

Help and contact

Email support@vicerobotics.com for anything about the Research Desk. A person reads every message.

Put one word in the subject

  • Accessibility for a barrier or accessibility feedback.
  • Security for a security problem. Our security.txt lists the same address.
  • Privacy for a privacy question or request.
  • Anything else needs no subject word.

You don’t need an account, and you don’t need to tell us about a disability.

How quickly we reply

“Acknowledge” means a reply from a person that confirms what you asked for and when you’ll hear from us next. An automatic reply doesn’t count.

Response times by type of request
RequestWe acknowledge withinThen
Security report2 business days. The same or next business day if it’s actively exploited or exposes user data.A first assessment within 5 business days, updates at least every 10 business days, and coordinated disclosure after 90 days or once fixed.
Accessibility barrier that blocks a task2 business daysOur first reply offers another way to finish the task where one exists. A fix plan or status within 10 business days, then updates every 10 business days.
Other accessibility feedback2 business daysA status within 10 business days.
Privacy request (access, correction, deletion, portability or opt-out)2 business daysCompleted within 30 calendar days. If we need more time, we explain why before day 30 and finish within 45 days of receiving it. Appeals are decided within 45 days.
General support2 business daysThe next update within 5 business days. We can’t promise a resolution time.

Business days are Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00 Eastern Time (America/New_York), excluding U.S. federal holidays. A message that arrives after 17:00 or on a non-business day counts as received the next business day. Calendar-day deadlines count from when we actually receive your message.

If we’re away, an automatic reply gives our return date. The deadlines still count from when we receive your message.

Privacy on this site

The Research Desk has no accounts. Your search and the optional comparison fields (rank, MOS, component, zone and years of service) are sent only to answer that request and aren’t stored by the application. Display settings are saved only in your browser.

Official sources

The Research Desk is an unofficial research aid. It isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by the United States Marine Corps. Always rely on the linked Marines.mil message as the authoritative source.
